Solo roll-and-write golf. Roll the die, aim your shot, navigate the terrain, and reach the hole in as few strokes as possible.

  • Aim: Tap a direction or tap the grid to aim that way
  • Swing: Roll the die for distance (1–6). Fairway = +1, Sand = −1
  • Putt: Move exactly 1 space — use near the hole
  • Water: +1 penalty stroke, ball returns to last spot
  • Trees: Shot blocked — ball stays put
  • Mulligans: 6 per round. Re-roll any shot
  • Tee shots: Re-rolls are free (no mulligan used)
