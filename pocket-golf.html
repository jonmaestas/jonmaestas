Pocket Golf

Real-time multiplayer golf. Create a clubhouse, share the code, play together on any device.

How to Play
Fairway: ×2
Rough: normal
Sand: ÷2
Water: +1
Trees: block
Green: putt
  • Tap anywhere on the grid to aim — full 360° control
  • Roll the die (1–6) for shot distance
  • Fairway: ×2 your roll — bomb it
  • Sand: ÷2 your roll (min 1)
  • Rough: roll as-is
  • Water: +1 penalty, ball goes back
  • Trees: shot blocked
  • Putt: 1 space toward the hole
  • Mulligans: 6 per round
