Real-time multiplayer golf. Create a clubhouse and share the code with your friends.

How to Play
Fairway: ×2
Rough: normal
Sand: ÷2
Water: +1
Trees: block
Green: putt
  • Roll the die (1–6) for shot distance
  • Fairway: ×2 your roll — bomb it down the middle
  • Sand: ÷2 your roll (min 1) — stuck in the bunker
  • Rough: roll as-is
  • Water: +1 penalty stroke, ball goes back
  • Trees: shot blocked, ball stays put
  • Green: putt = exactly 1 space
  • Aim: 8 directions, tap grid or arrows
  • Mulligans: 6 per round
