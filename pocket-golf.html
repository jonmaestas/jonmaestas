How to Play

Fairway: ×2
Rough: ×1
Sand: ÷2
Water: +1
Trees: block
Green: putt
  • Flick on the grid to shoot — direction + power come from your swipe
  • Drag slowly to aim, see the arrow and power bar
  • Release to fire. Longer/faster flick = more power
  • Fairway: ×2 power — bomb it down the middle
  • Sand: ÷2 power (min 1)
  • Water/OOB: +1 penalty, ball goes back
  • On the green: auto-putt toward hole, flick controls power
  • Mulligans: 6 per round
  • Play whenever — fully async between holes
  • Infinite Golf — global daily ladder, endless holes, same layout for everyone
