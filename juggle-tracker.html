📈 The 0-100 Progression

LEVEL 1 · 0–5 JUGGLES

Self-Drop

Hold ball at waist height. Drop it, kick it back to your hands with your laces. Catch. Repeat.

  • Start with your dominant foot
  • Kick straight up, not forward
  • Lock your ankle

Goal: 20 successful catches in a row

LEVEL 2 · 5–10 JUGGLES

Two Touches

Drop, kick up, let it bounce, kick up again, catch. The bounce gives you time to reset.

  • Same foot for both touches
  • Alternate: right-bounce-right, then left-bounce-left

Goal: 10 sets of 2 touches each foot

LEVEL 3 · 10–20 JUGGLES

Alternating Feet

Drop, kick right, let bounce, kick left, let bounce, repeat. Alternating feet is how you'll eventually juggle continuously.

  • Right-bounce-left-bounce pattern
  • Keep the ball at waist height

Goal: 10 alternating touches with bounces

LEVEL 4 · 20–50 JUGGLES

Remove the Bounces

Continuous juggles without letting the ball bounce. Start with 3 touches, then 5, then 10. Use whatever foot keeps the ball up.

  • Start from a self-drop
  • Keep touches soft and controlled
  • Stay on your toes, ready to move

Milestones: 5, 10, 20, 30, 50

LEVEL 5 · 50–100 JUGGLES

Add Thighs

When the ball gets too high or too close to your body, use your thigh. Thigh juggles give you time to reset.

  • Pattern: feet-feet-thigh-feet-feet
  • Angle your thigh to keep the ball going up
  • Use thigh touches to recover when off-balance

Goal: 100 touches using feet and thighs

LEVEL 6 · 100+ JUGGLES

Mastery

Once you can hit 100, you're ready for advanced juggling. Add head touches, juggle while walking, juggle with only your weak foot, do tricks.

⚠️ Common Mistakes

Mistake: Kicking the ball too high
Fix: Aim for waist height. Smaller, controlled touches give more control.
Mistake: Kicking forward instead of up
Fix: Point your toe down and kick straight up. The ball should land near where it started.
Mistake: Tense body and stiff movements
Fix: Relax. Stay light on your toes. Soft touches come from a relaxed body.
