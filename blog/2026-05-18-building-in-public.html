Building in Public

By Jon Maestas · May 18, 2026 · 1 min read

I've been posting on LinkedIn and X for years. Then it hit me: I don't own any of it.

Platforms change the rules, throttle your reach, or just die. Your content lives on someone else's servers, subject to their algorithm. Not great.
