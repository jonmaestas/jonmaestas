About Jon Maestas

Builder, parent-coach and founder of SmopLab LLC in Wright City, Missouri.

I live in Wright City, Missouri with my wife, Kristin, and our three daughters. They play softball, basketball, soccer and gymnastics, and I still coach them, even in the sports I don't fully know.

In high school I played baseball, basketball and football. I earned a baseball scholarship to Culver-Stockton College in Canton, Missouri, which is where I met Kristin.

I build software through my company, SmopLab LLC. Player 1 started because I wanted something in my pocket that looks at my kids' actual reps, not a random YouTube tip, between practices and lessons.

I write about building in public, AI agents and Athletic Intelligence on the blog.

What I build

  • Player 1: Athletic Intelligence: The free AI sports coach on your phone.
  • Player 1: Diamond Setup: Lay out a baseball or softball field with your iPhone.
  • Reindeer Food Co.: Reindeer Food for Christmas Eve.
  • Soccer Board: A 7v7 tactics board for coaches. Move players, draw runs, switch formations, and show Get Big vs. Squeeze.
  • Soccer Drill Timer: 45-second ball-mastery reps with self-paced rest. Say "go" to start the next rep, no hands needed.
  • Juggle Tracker: Counts soccer juggles with your phone's camera, with milestones and a 0 to 100 progression guide.
  • Pocket Golf: Flick-to-swing golf you play against friends, one turn at a time. Driver, iron and putter, and no luck in the lies.
  • Wright City Sports: Youth baseball, softball, T-ball, soccer, basketball and cheer in Wright City, Missouri, plus the Sports Closet for gear.
  • Coach's Playbook: Tips, practice plans, guides and videos for volunteer coaches. No experience required, and it installs like an app.
  • Game Day Status: Field and game status for sports events at a glance, like whether the baseball fields are open tonight.
  • Radio-to-web and voice-to-web: Turn a live radio show or any spoken broadcast into a searchable website, automatically: capture, transcribe, structure with AI and publish minutes after sign-off. Built and proven on a tradio swap-shop program west of St. Louis.
  • Custom AI and app builds: SmopLab LLC builds AI, apps and automation for problems without an off-the-shelf answer. Most projects start with a 72-hour prototype.

I also publish 18 free coaching guides for coaches, parents and players.
