Player 1: Diamond Setup New

Lay out a baseball or softball field with your iPhone.

Point the phone at the ground and Diamond Setup draws home plate, the pitching rubber and the bases in AR at your league's distances. Set up a bullpen or a whole diamond without a tape measure, then put the real bases on the outlines.

$4.99 one-time on the App Store (launch promo price). No subscription.

Product page · How to lay out a baseball or softball field

  • Bullpen layout for pitching practice, plus Full Field and Base by Base (beta).
  • Presets from 46′ Little League to 60′ 6″ high school and adult baseball, 35′ to 43′ fastpitch, slowpitch, or your own distances.
  • Mound height, feet or meters, and it works with no cell signal.
  • No account, no ads, no tracking. The camera view is never recorded or uploaded.
  • Any iPhone with iOS 18 or later. On LiDAR models the lines follow slopes in the ground.

Everyday tools

Beam

beam.jonmaestas.com

Move text and files between your devices with a QR code, right in the browser. No app, no account, and nothing is sent to a server.

  • Beam text

    Paste text and it plays as an animated QR code that your phone's camera reads.

  • Scanner

    Point the camera at a Beam code and get the text back, checked against a hash.

  • Share files

    Pair two devices with one scan, then send files up to 512 MB each, encrypted in the browser.
